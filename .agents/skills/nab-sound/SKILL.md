# Music, effects and mix

Use [AGENTS.md](../../../AGENTS.md) in the not-a-blogger checkout. Input: approved voice stems, scene/event
timings, intended mood and permitted sound sources. Preserve the spoken takes unless their repair is requested.

Use [sound recipes](../../../recipes/sound/README.md): effects follow events, musical changes serve the scene,
and every cut need not have the same whoosh. Record sources/licenses for external audio; generated or original
music also needs an accurate provenance record. Do not silently use a paid service or the author's private music.

Follow the project's existing mix command. Match the final target from
[QA-THRESHOLDS.md](../../../docs/QA-THRESHOLDS.md), distinguish take/master/final measurements and avoid
[soft-clipping the voice](../../../recipes/sound/sound-no-soft-clip-master.md). Measure the encoded final when
one is produced; a passing WAV does not establish the AAC result. Claim listening only when performed.

Output: stems or mixed audio, event placement, provenance and measured loudness/peak results, with unchanged-voice
identity and unperformed checks stated. If only a sound plan was requested, deliver a plan. Final video assembly,
speech regeneration, caption edits and publication are separate tasks.
