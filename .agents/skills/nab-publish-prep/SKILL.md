# Prepare publication materials

Use the not-a-blogger checkout and [AGENTS.md](../../../AGENTS.md). Input: exact video/hash, current QA,
target platforms/language, the person's links and actual media provenance. Keep the approved video unchanged.

Follow [chapter 08](../../../learn/en/08-vertical-publishing.md),
[publishing recipes](../../../recipes/publishing/README.md) and the
[delivery contract](../../../starter/DELIVERY.md). Prepare platform-appropriate titles/captions, relevant disclosures,
attribution and a checklist. Use the existing [credits utility](../../../tools/README.md) when its input is available;
missing author/license information is a gap, not invented provenance. Do not copy the toolkit author's channel,
brand or contact links into another person's video by default.

If drafting a queue/manifest, mark it as proposed and preserve ungranted artistic/publication approval. Identify
the exact file, targets, requested visibility and unresolved checks. Changing a caption or file after approval means
that proposal has changed; do not silently carry approval across versions.

Output: publication text and review package, clearly separate from a confirmed platform post. No account login,
upload, scheduling or public posting; no new publisher CLI is supplied by this preparation skill. Leave unrelated
rendering, speech, assets and software implementation alone.
