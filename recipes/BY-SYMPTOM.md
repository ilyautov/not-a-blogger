## Voice and TTS

- Picking or replacing a TTS engine. Don't decide from vendor demos or names; ASR barely separates engines and does not hear stress at all. → [Choose the TTS engine by a blind test with a numbered key](voice/voice-blind-tts-bakeoff.md) · proven
- The script uses dashes, ellipses, or colons for pauses, and F5-TTS reads the line without air. Worst with "meme" references, which hold no pauses. → [Make F5 pauses with a full stop or a hard marker, not a dash](voice/voice-f5-pauses.md) · proven
- The F5-TTS cloning wrapper prepends a lead token (Russian «Так,», "So,") and cuts it by ASR timestamps. The cut is imprecise: a vowel-initial line keeps a stray "k" («Отличие от файла», "The difference from the file", becomes «К отличии…»); a consonant-initial line loses its whole first word («Дальше про два дома», "Next, about two houses", becomes «Про два дома»); a line starting with the token word itself («Так что…», "So…") loses a real word. → [Make the first word of a line sacrificial and consonant-initial](voice/voice-f5-sacrificial-first-word.md) · proven
- F5-TTS clips the last sound of a line; mix ASR shows truncated words. The defect is in generation (the model did not finish), not at the segment boundary. → [Rescue the line ending with a sacrificial tail word and an alignment cut](voice/voice-f5-tail-sacrificial-word.md) · proven
- The timeline is laid out for the animation and gaps of 1.3–3.8 s appear between short lines. Or the opposite: every gap is the same constant, and the voice ticks like a metronome. → [Take gaps between lines from the script, short and uneven, and fit the scene mechanics to speech](voice/voice-gaps-between-lines.md) · proven
- A character with personality (boss, skit hero) needs a voice, and synthesis by description or a clone gives flat, "sad" prosody. → [Give the lead role to a live voice, keep synthesis for secondary characters](voice/voice-live-for-character.md) · hypothesis
- Someone (often an agent in another session) swaps the reference clip of a voice clone for a "cleaner" or "fresher" recording, or changes the speed or engine setting. Nobody asks the owner; a few videos later the voice "is not mine anymore". → [Don't change the clone's reference audio without a blind A/B by the voice owner; watch median pitch](voice/voice-lock-reference.md) · proven
- Takes differ in loudness (a calm reference synthesizes much quieter than an emotional one); the voice drowns under music and ASR errs. → [Normalise every take to −16 LUFS before mixing](voice/voice-loudness-per-take.md) · proven
- The clone sounds too slow for a reel and you are tempted to raise the model `speed` (e.g. to 1.15). → [Don't speed up the clone; get tempo from short phrases](voice/voice-no-speedup-clone.md) · proven
- Preparing a clone reference or a live voice-over. The clone copies everything in the reference, including clicks. → [Record the voice on a phone and remove mouth clicks before cloning](voice/voice-record-reference-clean.md) · proven
- A take has a clipped or garbled word and you are regenerating it repeatedly with a fixed seed. → [Fix a deterministic synthesis defect by editing text, not regenerating](voice/voice-seed-fix-by-text.md) · proven
- The Russian TTS model puts stress on the wrong syllable or hardens a consonant («модэль» for «модель», "model"; «флАгман», "flagship") or swallows an abbreviation. Stress marks `+`, doubled vowels, and hyphens work inconsistently. Per-sentence synthesis with even pauses flattens intonation. → [Fix wrong stress by replacing the word, not with marks; synthesize in chunks](voice/voice-stress-by-rewording.md) · proven
- You need a long, juicy shout and the TTS won't stretch the word. Caps and repeated letters barely help; `atempo 0.5` sounds distorted. → [Stretch a word with formant-preserving rubberband, not by spelling](voice/voice-stretch-word.md) · proven
- After cutting at the sacrificial tail word, some takes still contain it (the aligner swallowed the final interjection and the script logged "word not found, take left as is"). Or an engine clips the ending and the mix hides it. → [Check every take's tail and rescue uncut ones by energy](voice/voice-tail-rescue-check.md) · proven

## Camera

- Every shot opens with a push-in, a kick or a punch-in on a word. Each shot is fine alone; together the video pulses like one gesture repeated forty times, and the eye stops noticing the accents. → [Put camera accents on at most a third of the shots and never three push-ins in a row](camera/camera-accent-density.md) · hypothesis
- Scene elements arrive one by one while the camera holds the full 1920×1080 canvas: the first seconds of each scene show one line in empty space. Block bounding boxes lie (a 1280-wide card may hold a 600-wide line). → [Frame the camera on the actual text ink, not on the block's bounding box](camera/camera-follow-ink.md) · proven
- A static camera makes the frame dead; per-frame random offsets look like digital jitter; relative (`+=`) micro-hits on the same container as the camera shot conflict with it. → [Build handheld camera as a sum of sines on a separate layer](camera/camera-handheld-sines.md) · hypothesis
- A 1.35–1.5 push-in on a 1580-wide card pushes its edges off screen; a shot center right of 960 sends the left column off screen. After moving to vertical, push-ins that were safe at 1920 cut edge nodes in half, a diagram's outcome overflows the right edge. Half-cut cards at the edge read as a bug. → [Compute the camera shot with the screen formula so the hero never leaves the frame](camera/camera-keep-in-frame.md) · hypothesis
- A card hangs in frame with nothing happening inside, and a slow push-in over the whole scene is added. The frame technically moves; the viewer sees a slide. Stillness metrics are fooled too. → [Don't mask a static frame with a slow push-in](camera/camera-no-ken-burns.md) · proven
- In a 2D scene all layers shift equally and camera motion looks like sliding a flat picture. → [Move 2D layers by the depth formula dx = camX / z](camera/camera-parallax-formula.md) · hypothesis
- One shot scale for the whole video with a smooth zoom inside it; each new line looks like a new slide. → [Change shot scale with a cut per line instead of a six-percent zoom](camera/camera-scale-change-not-zoom.md) · hypothesis
