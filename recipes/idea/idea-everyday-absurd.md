## When
The topic is a tool and the draft is a dev tutorial ("how to install X") or a clip cut from a long video.

## Do
1. Use the formula "asked the AI to do X → absurd result": an everyday pain (call, email, calendar) plus an unexpected outcome.
2. Or place the story in a genre world: court, checkout, noir, messenger, pager, TV show. Put numbers into objects of that world (scoreboard, receipt, protocol), not into overlay cards.
3. Show the tool only at the end, as the fix. It is never the topic.

## Verify
Retell the video in one sentence without naming the tool. If you cannot, it is a tutorial: rewrite.

## Don't
- Don't force a "top-N" template onto a topic that is not a real list (keys, commands are fine). It produces slop.
- Don't open with the tool name.

## Evidence
- Snapshot of 39 YouTube Shorts: ceiling 1.0–1.3k views. Top: "asked the AI to free my Friday" (1,307) and a meme video with the best engagement (1,017 views, 28 likes). Tool how-tos 2–400, clips cut from a long video 1–17, long course retellings 32–79.
- Batch of ten videos in different genre worlds: five got the top rating from the author (spy noir, messenger, pager, court, checkout); "TV show" was "sluggish", one more "a bit boring, unclear".
- A tool tutorial video was rejected as "neuro-slop".
