## When
The next video is built on a successful example: your own hit, someone else's reference, the previous episode of a
series. The easy path keeps the structure, the camera and the ending and changes only the topic.

## Do
1. Before staging, write a "Reference and 4 of 6" field into the brief: the reference, then the six parameters for
   the reference and for the new video.

   | # | Parameter | Example of a difference |
   |---|---|---|
   | 1 | Structure | list → experiment; before/after → countdown |
   | 2 | Opening (first frame and first line) | result before the method instead of a question |
   | 3 | Signature frame (hero moment) | a grid of items → one item close up on paper |
   | 4 | Camera path | push-ins per line → one continuous follow of an object |
   | 5 | Music shape | flat bed → stop before the punchline and a hit after it |
   | 6 | Ending | spoken conclusion → visual punchline; loop → closed ending |

2. At least four must differ. Rethink the ones that match; don't just rename them.
3. Keep what the person approved for the series (voice, character, palette, captions): those are not counted, the
   other parameters carry the difference.
4. Before showing, put the new contact sheet next to the reference's sheet and recount on the frames, not on the plan.

## Verify
The brief has the six-row table with ≥ 4 rows marked "differs", and the side-by-side contact sheets confirm it: a
stranger can't mistake one video for the other.

## Don't
- Don't count a new topic or new words as a difference: none of the six is "topic".
- Don't change approved series elements to reach four; change structure, opening, hero frame, camera, music, ending.

## Evidence
- Viewer feedback: a short made as a copy of the author's successful short on the same skeleton, with a new topic,
  was rejected by the person as slop. A sequel on the same template published right after the original got 3 views
  ([no sequel on the same template](idea-no-repeat-own-hit.md)).
- The threshold "4 of 6" is not measured by the author; it comes from lemomo-ai/lemo-opuscar (MIT), `DIRECTOR.md`.
  To promote: compare the person's verdicts on videos with ≤ 3 and ≥ 4 differing parameters.
