## When
The video is about a tool (voice clone, text humanizer) and the draft is an install guide.

## Do
1. Make the viewer a participant: "guess which voice is mine", "these words give away AI text".
2. Reveal the answer at the end, ideally with a twist (e.g. "the whole video was the clone"), only if the twist is true.
3. Put the how-to in the last seconds or behind a code word.

## Verify
The first sentence is a question or challenge to the viewer, not the tool name.

## Don't
Don't repeat unverified claims from other hits ("100 % human"). The exposé must be honest.

## Evidence
- Voice-clone videos on YouTube: guessing game median 3.7k, how-to 787.
- AI-text videos: "I spot AI text instantly" exposés reach 370–750k for Western authors and 430k for a Russian-language one; install tutorials 4k or less.
- Own video in the "I spot AI text" format: 989 views on YouTube and 530 on TikTok in 24 h, with a channel ceiling around 1.3k.
