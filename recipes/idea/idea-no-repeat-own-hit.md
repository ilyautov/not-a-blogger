## When
A video worked and the next one reuses its background, card, character bubble, and line formulas.

## Do
1. After a hit, look for a new live situation, not new text for the old template.
2. Never publish two versions of the same video back to back.
3. Give each video in a series its own visual world; share only the character and the refrain.

## Verify
Put a frame of the new video next to a frame of the previous one. If they can be confused, the template is the same: redo.

## Don't
Don't confuse this with a series format: one template works when the situation inside changes, not just the card topic.

## Evidence
- Second version posted right after the first: 3 views on YouTube, 0 on TikTok.
- Third video in a row on one template was called "slop"; the next batch of five with different visual worlds got three top ratings, one "format OK", one "a bit boring".
