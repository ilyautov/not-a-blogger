## When
The video feels like a presentation no matter how much animation it has. Layout gives the slide away: a title on top repeating the voice, a diagram in the middle, a bar/footer/name at the bottom, a new composition per line with a crossfade, picture in one zone and diagrams in another.

## Do
1. An illustration fills the whole frame or is absent.
2. One continuous shot with a hero the viewer follows.
3. No titles: only short lower-third captions that appear after the word.
4. No chrome: no bars, footer, name or watermark. Logo only at the very end.
5. A change of thought = cut + shot scale change, not a new composition with a transition.
6. Check "how many zones in frame", not "how much motion". More than one = slide. Check before building the template.

## Verify
Take the middle frame of each scene and count zones: separate "header", "picture" and "footer" = slide.

## Don't
Don't apply to screen-based top-N formats (shortcuts, commands): there a two-zone layout "character circle + terminal + live background" beat the full-screen cut version. The one-zone rule is for story videos.

## Evidence
- The same complaint ("feels like a presentation", "the picture hangs and gets in the way") repeated four times across videos while the layout stayed two-zone; animation inside zones did not remove it.
- A video where half the frames were tables and bars: "everything is very bad".
