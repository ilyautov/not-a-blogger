## When
A story about a real news event tempts you to generate its participants (a person from the news, a famous character).

## Do
1. Never realistically generate real third parties.
2. Make recognisable types as archetypes: "mafia boss", not a specific actor's face.
3. Label event reconstructions "reconstruction".
4. Use explicit stylisation (clay, low-poly, cartoon): it removes likeness issues and needs no AI label on YouTube.
5. A real face only if it is your own or with written consent.

## Verify
No realistic face of a non-consenting person in frame; every reconstruction has a label.

## Don't
Don't rely on remembered platform rules: check current AI-label requirements before publishing.

## Evidence
No measurements: rule taken from YouTube's AI-label help and an analysis of generative formats. Platforms require an AI label for realistic content with a real person.
