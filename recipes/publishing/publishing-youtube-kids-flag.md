## When
The video went out with audience "Made for kids". YouTube then disables comments and notifications. A "comment the word" CTA fails, and from outside it looks like a normal video.

## Do
1. Once, in channel settings (Studio → Settings → Channel → Advanced settings) choose "No, set this channel as not made for kids" instead of "review this setting for every video".
2. After every upload open Studio → Content ("Shorts" tab for vertical): the row must not say "Comments off", the restrictions column must not say "Made for kids".
3. If flagged: video → Details → Audience → "No, it's not made for kids" → Save. Comments reopen.

## Verify
Open the video logged out: the comment field is present.

## Don't
- Don't remove the flag from a video genuinely for kids: platform rules require it, and comments stay closed.

## Evidence
In a series of five videos (one long, four Shorts) all five got the kids flag. Before that, five more (one Short and four cuts) did the same. Each time the flag was removed manually after publishing.
