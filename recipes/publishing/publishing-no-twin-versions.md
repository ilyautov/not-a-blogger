## When
A new version came out better (different background, codeword ending) and you want to post it right away. On our channel such a second version got almost no views; why is not established (a platform duplicate filter is one guess, the first version was never taken down and there was no control).

## Do
1. As a precaution, don't publish the improved version right after the original. Release it several videos later with a different hook. Deleting the original and re-uploading was never tested as a fix.
2. Before every upload check the published registry (file, platform, link, date) to avoid a duplicate.

## Verify
The registry has no two consecutive entries of the same story on one platform.

## Don't
- Don't count a remake with a different hook, scene and example as a duplicate.

## Evidence
The second version, uploaded right after the first, got 3 views on YouTube and 0 on TikTok. The ceiling of the channel's regular Shorts in that period was about 1.0–1.3k views. One case, cause unknown: kept as a hypothesis.
