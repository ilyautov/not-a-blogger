## When
The Reels wizard changes: for a ready 9:16 vertical the crop screen no longer appears, and the "Add AI label" toggle hides and isn't always found. A video with synthesized voice or generated imagery goes out unlabeled.

## Do
1. Upload manually from the app or browser.
2. On the last wizard screen open "Advanced settings" and enable "AI label" if the video has synthesized voice, generated frames or a face.
3. Add a caption line stating what was made by AI (voice, image), even if the label is set.
4. After publishing open the video by direct link and check title, caption, links.

## Verify
The AI label is visible on the video page; the caption has the disclosure.

## Don't
- Don't rely on exact menu names: Instagram changes its UI without notice.

## Evidence
No own measurement. In four recorded uploads the label toggle wasn't found in the wizard, so the AI disclosure stayed in the caption.
