# Publishing

Manual and verification steps for uploading to YouTube, TikTok and Instagram. No account-login automation here. Format: [recipe template](../../templates/recipe.md).

| Recipe | Status | Lang | Gist |
|---|---|---|---|
| [publishing-failed-click-is-draft](publishing-failed-click-is-draft.md) | hypothesis | any | Dead "Publish" click or "processing longer" = draft; >180 s vertical is in Videos. |
| [publishing-instagram-ai-label](publishing-instagram-ai-label.md) | hypothesis | any | Enable the AI label in advanced settings and disclose in the caption. |
| [publishing-long-before-shorts](publishing-long-before-shorts.md) | hypothesis | any | Shorts go out only after the long video's link is live. |
| [publishing-no-twin-versions](publishing-no-twin-versions.md) | hypothesis | any | On our channel a second version right after the first got almost no views (cause unknown). |
| [publishing-tiktok-only-me](publishing-tiktok-only-me.md) | proven | any | "Only me" after publishing clears in minutes; check Studio, don't re-upload. |
| [publishing-wait-upload-100](publishing-wait-upload-100.md) | hypothesis | any | Keep the Studio tab open until "Uploaded 100 %". |
| [publishing-youtube-kids-flag](publishing-youtube-kids-flag.md) | proven | any | Check and clear "Made for kids" after every upload. |
| [publishing-youtube-daily-upload-cap](publishing-youtube-daily-upload-cap.md) | proven | any | ~11th upload of the day is blocked for 24 h, no draft; a ceiling, not a plan. |
| [publishing-first-three-slots](publishing-first-three-slots.md) | hypothesis | any | One small channel: YouTube uploads 1–3 of a day ~970 views vs 9 for 4+; TikTok drop after ~5. Cause unknown; ≤3 / ≤5 a day as a precaution. |
