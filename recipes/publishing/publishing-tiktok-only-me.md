## When
Right after publishing TikTok shows "Content under review" and privacy "Only me". It looks like an error, you re-upload, and the account gets two copies.

## Do
1. Wait a few minutes after publishing.
2. Check status in TikTok Studio → Content, not the profile (the profile lags).
3. Re-upload only if the status hasn't changed after half an hour and Studio shows an explicit rejection.

## Verify
In TikTok Studio the video's privacy is "Everyone" and views are growing.

## Don't
- Don't verify ownership via public oEmbed: it no longer returns the author link; check in Studio.
- Don't wait forever on a real moderation failure: the status won't change and Studio shows a notice with the reason.

## Evidence
In two recorded cases the video first sat in "Under review / Only me" and moved to "Everyone" by itself within minutes. Neither needed a re-upload.
Sample size 2 (two uploads, both cleared by themselves). A third case that did not clear would send this back to hypothesis.
