## When
A post got tens of thousands of impressions and is logged as a success. If nobody clicked the link or subscribed, the reach brought nothing.

## Do
1. For every post record the full funnel, not the first number:

```text
impressions -> profile views -> link clicks -> subscriptions/leads
```

2. If clicks are zero for several measurements in a row, look for a broken mechanism, not bad content: is there a clickable link, where does it lead, does the post say why to follow it.

## Verify
Every post row has all four stages; a post with zero at the last stage has a recorded reason.

## Don't
- Don't trust platforms that hide clicks from the author: use a UTM-tagged link or your own page and count visits on your side.

## Evidence
Top-reach post of the month: 19,123 impressions → 35 profile views (0.18 %) → 0 followers, 0 link clicks. Another report by the same author: 3,261 impressions → 43 article views → 0 and 0. In total more than 13k impressions over six consecutive measurements gave 0 clicks; the profile had no clickable channel link.
