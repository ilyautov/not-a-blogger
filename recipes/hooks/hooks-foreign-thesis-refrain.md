## When
A faceless video sounds like an anonymous narrator: nothing to argue about in comments, no recognisable signature.

## Do
1. Hook = a confident claim from the niche: a 2–6 s clip of another video or a post screenshot ("paste this prompt and…").
2. Short reply: "I can comment. Nonsense. Let me show you."
3. Prove it with a live run on screen.
4. End every video with the same refrain line. It signs the video better than a brand card.
5. Prefer topics that split viewers into camps (one model vs another).

## Verify
The refrain is identical across the series; the hook claim is a real quote with a source link, not invented.

## Don't
- Don't invent the opponent's claim.
- Keep the quoted clip short and credited, within quotation norms.

## Evidence
Breakdown of an author with a 311k median views per video:
- opponent-claim hook in 6–7 of 8 hits;
- the same closing line in all 11 transcripts;
- "camp" topics give 1.4× more comments per view.
That author shows a live face and voice; untested without a face.
