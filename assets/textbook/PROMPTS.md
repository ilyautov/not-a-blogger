# Textbook illustrations: prompts

Every picture got the two style references (an earlier illustration of the same series) and this shared header:

```text
Input images 1 and 2 are STYLE AND CHARACTER REFERENCES from the user's own illustrated series; NOT scene templates. Match the exact hand-inked editorial comic look: crisp dark outlines, detailed tufts of fur, light paper grain, layered cel shading, pale daylight interior in warm white and grey, plum-purple Goro and sparse fluorescent lime accents. Everything drawn; no photography, no glossy 3D, no cyberpunk.
GORO: stocky large gorilla, tufted dark plum crown, broad pale grey flat face, heavy eyebrows, small amber eyes, charcoal short-sleeved collared button-up shirt, large grey-purple hands, focused dry skeptical expression. CHIMP assistant: smaller brown-black chimp, beige face, white shirt, slim black tie. Anatomically coherent hands, clear gestures, no extra limbs. No helmets, no robots.
No lettering, words, numbers, logos, speech bubbles, captions, UI text or watermarks anywhere. One coherent physical scene with foreground/midground/background depth, readable at 700px display width. Do not reproduce parcels, ribbons or delivery backpacks from the references.
```

Files from 23.10.2026 reuse illustrations made for an earlier guide: ch00 (film workshop), ch01 (storyboard), ch03 (voice), ch05 (editing), ch06 (sound), ch07 (review). Their scenes follow the same header.

## cover

a compact daylight home studio, the opening image of the whole textbook. Goro sits at a sturdy wooden desk, holding a smartphone in a small handheld gimbal, filming a single ceramic mug standing on a tiny tabletop set under a desk lamp. The phone screen faces the viewer slightly and shows the same mug in a vertical frame. The chimp assistant leans in from the right with an open laptop whose screen shows only simple abstract coloured blocks, eagerly pointing at the mug. Behind them: a pinboard with three pictorial storyboard sketches, a small microphone on a stand, a coiled cable. Lime only on the gimbal grip, the mic cable and one pin. Calm, confident, wry mood: two friends running a tiny video factory.

## ch00a

a tabletop machine made of five simple physical stations connected by one conveyor belt, seen in three-quarter view: a stack of blank script pages, a microphone, a small camera, a speaker box, and at the end a vertical phone-shaped frame where a finished tiny scene with a mug appears. The chimp assistant on the left feeds a blank page into the first station, Goro on the right stands at the end of the belt holding a magnifying glass over the finished vertical frame, checking it with a skeptical eyebrow. Each station is clearly separate and readable, like a diagram brought to life. Lime only on the conveyor belt edge and one indicator light.

## ch02

an editing desk. The chimp assistant proudly unrolls an absurdly long paper scroll covered in dense meaningless squiggle lines and ornamental flourishes that spills off the desk and coils on the floor. Goro, seated, calmly holds a lime marker and has already crossed out most of the scroll with bold strokes; with his other hand he holds up a small clean index card with only three short squiggle lines on it. The joke: a bloated text turned into a short human note. A wastebasket overflowing with crumpled paper in the foreground, soft window light.

## ch04

a small photo studio. A large empty wooden vertical frame (tall portrait proportion, like a phone screen) stands on an easel in the centre. Goro stands behind it, holding his two hands in a framing gesture, carefully composing so that a single big object, a ceramic mug on a stool, fills most of the vertical frame. The chimp assistant at the side holds a round white reflector and is about to bring in a pile of extra props (a lamp, a plant, a clock), which Goro stops with a raised palm. The idea: one hero object fills the frame, nothing extra. Lime only on the reflector rim and a tape mark on the floor.

## ch08

a workshop table. The chimp assistant struggles to push a wide horizontal landscape painting through a narrow tall vertical slot in a wooden box, clearly not fitting, edges bending. Next to him Goro calmly rebuilds the same picture for the vertical format: he has cut the painting's elements (a mug, a lamp, a small figure) into separate paper pieces and is rearranging them, stacked vertically, inside a tall portrait frame. Three phone stands with vertical screens wait at the back of the table. The idea: re-layout, not crop. Lime only on a cutting mat edge and the phone stand clips.

## ch09

a bright room with a long washing line stretched across, holding a sequence of large square cards pinned with clothes pegs, each card a simple pictorial drawing that continues the previous one (a mug, the mug tipping, the mug spilling, a clean mug again). Goro stands at the left end reviewing the sequence with arms crossed, one eyebrow raised, the chimp assistant at the right end is pinning the last card and swiping the air with one finger as if scrolling a carousel. A table below with a paper cutter and blank squares. Lime only on the clothes pegs.

## ch10

an office corner. Goro sits at a desk handing the chimp assistant a single short sheet of paper with a few simple pictograms (no text). The chimp, standing, returns a neat cardboard box with the lid open. Goro is holding a clipboard with an empty checklist of three square boxes and is looking inside the box with a magnifying glass, not taking the assistant's thumbs-up at face value. The chimp gives an over-confident thumbs-up. The idea: give a clear brief, then check the result yourself. Lime only on the clipboard clip and one box tape.
