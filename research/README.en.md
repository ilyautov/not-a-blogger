# Research

Anonymized conclusions from our research: what takes off in short AI videos, which tools to pick, what generation
costs. Named tables of other people's videos, breakdowns of specific creators and data dumps are not here and won't be.

| File | What's inside |
|---|---|
| [findings.en.md](findings.en.md) | 20 findings: the hit metric, comments vs likes, the lifespan of a format, the QC stack, the script as a chain of knowledge |
| [formats.en.md](formats.en.md) | 11 formats that take off, cross-cutting amplifiers and anti-formats |
| [landscape.en.md](landscape.en.md) | neighboring open-source projects with licenses and differences |
| [tts-bakeoff.en.md](tts-bakeoff.en.md) | a blind test of six TTS engines in Russian |

## How to contribute your own research

1. Describe the method: where the sample comes from, how many videos, how you selected hits, the snapshot date.
2. Give a conclusion with a number and its limits ("correlation, no completion data").
3. Don't publish handles, names or tables of other people's videos. A link to a public example video is fine; a breakdown of a specific person is not.
4. Collect data only by reading public pages, without other people's sessions, cookies or bypassing platform restrictions.
