# "They said the same about DeepSeek"

![Frame from the video](poster.jpg)

YouTube Shorts: https://youtube.com/shorts/d5TUJTQ2RXY (released 17.09.2026, third version).

## Format and length

Vertical explainer, 1080×1920, 59 s. Dark stage, one hero object in frame (a stack of "glass"
headlines), camera push-ins, karaoke captions. Synthesized voice. Genre: taking apart a hype
headline ("China beat GPT again").

## Hook (first line)

«Китай опять обошёл GPT. Слышал? Я это слышу каждые пару месяцев.» [China beat GPT again. Heard
that? I hear it every couple of months.]

## Second-by-second structure

| Time | What you hear | On screen |
|---|---|---|
| 0.3–5.6 | Hook: «Китай опять обошёл GPT» [China beat GPT again] | A stack of news headlines from a year and a half |
| 5.6–10.6 | January 2025, DeepSeek, the NVIDIA stock drop in a day | News card |
| 10.6–17.4 | Where things stood a year and a half later: ChatGPT and DeepSeek traffic shares | Share comparison |
| 17.4–24.1 | Catch number one: a single in-house test | Camera pushes into the small print of a benchmark table |
| 24.1–30.6 | Catch number two: compared against an outdated version | Card with the version crossed out |
| 30.6–37.9 | Catch number three: someone else's numbers from the internet | Table with a "source: someone else's table" badge |
| 37.9–47.4 | What's actually true: DeepSeek is much cheaper and takes a large share of tokens on OpenRouter | The price number big, then the share number |
| 47.4–52.5 | «Заголовок про качество. Реальность про цену.» [The headline is about quality. The reality is about price.] | «обошёл» [beat] fades, «дешевле» [cheaper] lights up |
| 52.5–59 | «Не согласен? Напиши в комментариях слово ИИ. Поспорим.» [Disagree? Write the word "AI" in the comments. Let's argue.] | Comment field with the word «ИИ» [AI] |

The market numbers the video quotes (the stock drop, traffic shares, price, token share) are left out of this
breakdown: the primary sources aren't recorded in the kit.

## Techniques used

- [Open with someone else's confident claim and close with your own refrain](../../recipes/hooks/hooks-foreign-thesis-refrain.md): a hook with someone else's headline plus recognition ("I hear it every couple of months")
  puts the author on the viewer's side.
- Three "catches" with the same opening phrase: the viewer knows how many are left (no recipe yet).
- Camera push-ins into small print as a visual metaphor for "read the fine print" (no recipe yet).
- Numbers big across the whole frame (no recipe yet).
- [Keep one open loop and close it at the end](../../recipes/hooks/hooks-open-loop-payoff.md): a contrarian conclusion closes the hook's question; the headline is about one thing, the reality about another.
- [End a reel with a question and a code word the viewer writes in the comments](../../recipes/cta/cta-codeword-in-comments.md): an argument CTA, "disagree? write the word".
- [Fix stress by rewording, not with marks, and synthesize in chunks](../../recipes/voice/voice-stress-by-rewording.md): words the synthesizer reads with the wrong stress were replaced with other words,
  not stress marks.

## What worked

- The channel's author accepted the visual grammar (one hero, camera push-ins, karaoke):
  «визуал окей, скорость плюс-минус» [visuals OK, pace so-so]. It was later used as the model
  for explainers.
- The topic is news, but the conclusions don't go stale within a week.
- One of the channel's three best shorts on YouTube as of the measurement date.

## What didn't work

- Three versions because of the voiceover. The word «дырка» [hole] sounded bad and was replaced
  with «подвох» [catch]. Stress marks and doubled vowels didn't work in the synthesizer for
  either «флагман» [flagship] or «модель» [model]: both words had to be cut from the script.
- 59 seconds is borderline for an explainer: the plan was about 45 s.
- The code word «ИИ» [AI] in YouTube comments doesn't work as a delivery mechanism (there are
  no DMs there).

## How to repeat it

1. Take a headline that keeps repeating in the news.
2. Find 3 concrete catches with sources (in-house test, old version, someone else's numbers).
3. Make one hero object for the frame (a stack of headlines, a document, a screen) and move the
   camera across it.
4. End with "it's actually about something else" and an argument-starting question.
5. Before synthesis, run the script for words with risky stress and replace them in advance.
